# M&T Uniforms project intent

## Client-first modernization

The first outcome is to understand and modernize the business workflow M&T
Uniforms already operates: preserve its data, restore reliable ordering,
document the handoffs between storefront, counter sales, invoicing, and staff,
and remove avoidable friction. Do not turn unverified ecommerce features into
requirements.

New capabilities — including per-officer allowances, authorization codes,
agency portals, automated approvals, and custom operational systems — are a
separate second phase. Propose them only after the current workflow is observed
through authenticated exports/admin access and discussed with the client.

## Communication

Explain ecommerce concepts in plain language as they arise. Distinguish what is
observed on the current site, what the client has stated, and what remains an
assumption or possible future feature.
