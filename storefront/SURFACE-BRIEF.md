# Storefront surface brief

- **Mode:** Operate with a persuasive opening.
- **Audience:** Public-safety, postal, corrections, security, organizational, and local buyers; secondary audience is M.T. Uniforms staff receiving the request.
- **Job:** Find an item, resolve fit and customization choices, preserve them, and reach a human.
- **Primary action:** Add a configured item to the request, then draft an email or call.
- **Proof/content:** Seven products and exact images from the REC-015 public snapshot, plus the exact order-continuity notice.
- **Direction:** Quartermaster Order Ticket, surface seed `734cebaa`; first comp selected, with search-first language and the compact category shelf carried from alternates.
- **Memorable moment:** The interface behaves like one continuous supply-counter ticket from role to fit to human confirmation.
- **Boundary:** Prototype only. No payment, submission, inventory promise, private data, or approved final identity.
