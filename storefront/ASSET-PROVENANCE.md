# Storefront asset provenance

The seven product images are exact public binaries copied from the REC-015 recovery package at `public-site/media/`. Their SHA-256 values are enforced by `scripts/verify.mjs`. They support a stakeholder prototype and migration reference; they do not prove current inventory, price, availability, or image rights.

The recovered 299 by 82 historical logo is retained in `public/assets` as reference and is not rendered as the proposed identity. The storefront uses a CSS concept monogram so no unapproved replacement logo is presented as final.

Archivo is copied from the existing brand gallery with its license beside it. Product sources and recovered source-image URLs are recorded per fixture in `src/data.ts`.
