# MT Uniforms Brand Direction Gallery

## Product truth

- Audience: MT Uniforms owners and project stakeholders choosing a new brand direction.
- Job: compare three concept systems, understand the recommendation, and see a client-safe snapshot of rebuild progress.
- Primary action: review the three boards and choose a direction to advance.
- Authority: the three 2026-08-08 brand-direction boards and their adjacent rationale; project status comes from the current Work Scope state and `MAP.md`.
- Public boundary: publish no passwords, customer records, internal paths, hashes, credential mechanics, or private recovery contents.
- Status language: recovery groundwork is verified; private OpenCart and Ecwid exports remain; the temporary ordering notice is still pending; platform selection remains separate.
- Brand language: every direction is exploratory and requires deterministic vector redraw, small-size testing, and trademark review before production use.

## Experience brief

The gallery is a calm selection room, not a fourth MT Uniforms identity. A compact project-state strip establishes context, then the boards take over at editorial scale. Quartermaster leads as the recommendation, with Service Standard and One Mission presented as credible alternatives rather than equal template cards.

## Technical boundary

- Static HTML, CSS, and minimal JavaScript.
- No runtime database, analytics, forms, or authentication.
- Deploy as the Vercel project `mt-uniforms-brand-directions`.
- Responsive and keyboard-accessible, with reduced-motion support.
